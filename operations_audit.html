Quick Next Steps

  1. Pick 1–2 processes that consistently cause delays or frustration.
  2. Identify the root cause: manual entry, unclear ownership, tool mismatch, or missing data.
  3. Test a small automation or redesign (pilot with one team) and measure time saved.
  4. Iterate and scale what works; document processes and owners as you go.
