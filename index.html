I map back-office systems and build the fixes for companies whose real data lives in someone's spreadsheet… or their head.

Orders get printed and walked to the floor. Bills get retyped into a tracker. The numbers that matter live with one person, and nobody has mapped how data moves between your systems.

I start by talking to the executives and to the people doing the work in each department. Then I recommend what to fix, replace or leave alone, and I build what needs building.

Most engagements start with a fixed-fee tech stack assessment or a short discovery sprint, then move to a monthly retainer for the build. By day 90 there's a working tool in production.

For a dental device manufacturer, I built an FDA safety search in a month. A search that took the regulatory lead and an intern about two weeks per submission now takes a couple of hours to run and review.

Most of what I build runs on plain queries and rules. If a script solves the problem, you get a script.

Before L'Optimist I was a data scientist, product manager and engagement manager at a health data company. MS in Applied Mathematics, BA from the University of Chicago.
